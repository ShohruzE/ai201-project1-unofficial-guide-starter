What is LinkedIn?
LinkedIn is the Instagram of professionals. You will be be able to meet people all around the world who already work in careers that you’re interested in and other college students who are in the same place as you.

Why LinkedIn?
Taking the time to create a LinkedIn profile will give you access to a plethora of benefits, such as job opportunities, learning from professionals and their experiences, speaking with recruiters to potentially make you stand out from other applicants and so much more. You won’t believe me until you see it for yourself.

Get Connecting
However, creating the perfect LinkedIn takes time and is necessary to make yourself stand out from the crowd. There isn’t one perfect video but [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B4OhuzwLc9o) one from Jeff Su covers the most bases and helps you get started and more familiar with the platform.

We recommend you watch more than 1 video on creating a strong profile as it can help familiarize yourself with the platform.

Instead of swiping away on Instagram and TikTok, do the same on LinkedIn and learn more about the professional world. You are going to learn so many things that you didn’t before and you might be shocked by the information that’s on there.

As college students, we need to start taking more initiative because graduation will be here before we know it. Instead of feeling the excitement of ending college and going on into your professional career, you will feel worried that you don’t have a job yet and perhaps LinkedIn was the reason why.

You won't regret it.
