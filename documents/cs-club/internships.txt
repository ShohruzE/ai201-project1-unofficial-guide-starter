Internships
Now that you've created your resume and done some interview prep, you can apply to internships!

Generally, lowerclassmen such as freshmen and sophomores have decreased chances of getting an interview as upperclassmen typically have more extensive knowledge and, therefore are more likely to succeed in the company. However, don't let that discourage you.

It does not hurt to apply to any applications because you will never know when a recruiter will give you a chance. If you are given the chance, use these interviews as experience to prepare you for future interviews, thus you will be much more aware of previous mistakes and won't make them again.

Current Internships
Here is a [GitHub](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2026-Internships) repository that lists many tech internships for the current/upcoming summer. This gets updated every summer so be sure to save it for future reference!

Lowerclassman?
If you are an underclassman, don't worry because there are special internships/programs catered for you. These internships typically encourage underrepresented minorities to apply, so don't be afraid!
